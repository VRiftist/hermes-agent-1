## KEY INSIGHT: The "AI Tax" is Real
Users are willing to pay MORE for AI, but only if it's clearly valuable:
- Notion AI: $10/mo ADD-ON (on top of $10 base) = $20/mo total = $240/yr
- Craft AI: $9.99/mo ADD-ON = nearly doubles cost
- Capacities: $8/mo INCLUDES AI — perceived better value

## Revenue Maximization Strategy

### Option A: Pure Freemium (Recommended for Max Adoption)
- **Free**: Local-only, basic editing, max 50 notes, no AI, 2K context
- **Starter**: $8/mo (or $60/yr) — AI compose, auto-tag, 8K context, 500 notes
- **Pro**: $20/mo (or $180/yr) — All AI, 16K context, unlimited notes, memory palace, priority models
- **Enterprise**: $50/user/mo (or $450/yr) — Teams, admin, API, custom models

**Math (Pro tier focus):**
- 1,000 Pro users = $20,000/mo = $240,000/yr 💰
- 10,000 Pro users = $200,000/mo = $2.4M/yr
- 50,000 total users, 5% convert = 2,500 Pro → $50K/mo

**Why this works:**
- $8 starter is impulse-buy territory (less than Netflix)
- $20 Pro is competitive with Notion AI total
- Free tier creates massive funnel
- Annual discount (10% vs monthly) = better cash flow + retention

### Option B: Aggressive Launch Pricing
- **Free**: Same as above but 100 notes, 3K context, 1 AI action/day
- **Starter**: $5/mo (launch) → $8/mo (normal) — first 6 months
- **Pro**: $12/mo (launch) → $20/mo (normal) — first 6 months
- **Why**: 50% discount drives 3-5x adoption spike. Users who start 
  during launch discount rarely churn when price increases.
- **Risk**: Revenue delayed, but 10x user base = viral growth

### Option C: Usage-Based (Higher Revenue Ceiling)
- **Free**: Unlimited notes, but pay-per-AI-action
- **AI credits**: $1 per 100 AI actions, $5/month for 1,000
- **Pro unlimited**: $25/mo
- **Why**: Users who barely use AI pay almost nothing → huge free funnel. 
  Power users who LIVE in AI pay more. Matches value to cost.
- **Risk**: Complexity, user confusion, billing complaints

## RECOMMENDATION: Hybrid (Option A + B combined)

### Final Pricing Structure

| Tier | Monthly | Annual (15% off) | What You Get |
|------|---------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Free** | $0 | $0 | Local only, basic notes, 50 notes, no AI |
| **Starter** | $8 | $60/yr | AI compose, auto-tag, 8K context, 500 notes |
| **Pro** | $20 | $180/yr | All AI, unlimited notes, 16K, memory palace |
| **Enterprise** | $50/user | custom | Teams, admin, API, priority models |

### Launch Strategy (Year 1)
- **Months 1-3**: Pro at $12/mo (40% off) — early adopters
- **Months 4-6**: Pro at $16/mo (20% off) — growth phase
- **Month 7+**: Full pricing ($20/mo) — steady state
- **Annual bundle**: Always 15% off (not 50% — protects revenue)
- **Lifetime deal consideration**: $399 one-time for early supporters

### Why NOT $200-300/mo (Claude's suggestion)
- Claude's $200-300/mo targets enterprise ONLY
- For personal PKM, this is too high — users will resist
- Better: $20/mo personal Pro + $50/user/mo enterprise
- This captures both markets without pricing out individuals

## Key Metrics to Track
1. **Conversion rate**: Free → Starter (target: 5-8%)
2. **Conversion rate**: Starter → Pro (target: 15-20%)
3. **Monthly churn**: Track by tier (target: <5% for Pro)
4. **AI usage per user**: Actions per day/week
5. **Revenue per user (ARPU)**: Track overall and by tier
6. **Lifetime value (LTV)**: Pro user LTV should exceed 12 months
7. **Net promoter score (NPS)**: User satisfaction pre/post pricing
8. **Feature usage heatmap**: Which AI features drive conversion?
