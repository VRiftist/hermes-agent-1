## Final Pricing Structure

| Tier | Launch Price (Year 1) | Full Price (Year 2+) | Annual Equivalent | What You Get |
|------|----------------------|---------------------|-------------------|-------------|
| **Free** | $0 | $0 | $0 | Local only, basic notes, no AI, 50 notes, 2K context |
| **Starter** | $5/mo (50% off) | $8/mo | $72/yr | AI compose, auto-tag, 8K context, 500 notes |
| **Pro** | **$10/mo (50% off)** | **$20/mo** | **$180/yr** | **All 7 skills, 16K context, unlimited notes, memory palace, priority models** |
| **Enterprise** | **$50/user/mo** | **$100/user/mo** | Custom | Teams, admin, API, custom skills, priority support |

### Why 50% Off (Not 40%)

- The gap between $10 and $20 isn't a dealbreaker — it's a "barely a coffee" vs "two coffees"
- At $10, you're in the impulse-buy zone. People subscribe without asking their manager
- By month 3, they have 200+ organized notes and a working memory palace
- Month 6: the idea of going back to Notion feels like giving up a superpower
- When the bill goes from $10 → $20, the response is "Oh, that's it?" not "Cancel!"
- Annual discount at full price: 10% off ($18/mo effective) — just enough to reward commitment without gutting revenue

### Revenue Model Math

#### Conservative (Year 1)
- 2,000 launch users, 8% conversion to paid = 160 paying users
- 100 Starter ($5/mo) + 60 Pro ($10/mo) = $800/mo = $9,600/yr
- This is seed-stage money. The point is adoption, not revenue.

#### Year 2 (Price increases, retention compounds)
- 60% retention from Year 1 = 96 users at full price
- Plus organic growth: 4,000 total users, 12% paid = 480 paying
- 250 Starter ($8/mo) + 230 Pro ($20/mo) = $6,600/mo = $79,200/yr
- Enterprise: 5 teams × 10 users × $100/mo = $5,000/mo = $60,000/yr
- **Total Year 2: ~$139K/yr**

#### Year 3 (Flywheel)
- Users who've been on Hermes for 2+ years are not leaving
- 8,000 users, 15% paid = 1,200 paying
- Revenue: ~$360K/yr + Enterprise deals
- Custom skill marketplace takes 15% cut → additional revenue stream

## The Lock-In Mechanics (Built Into the Product)

1. **Memory Palace grows over time** — switching means losing accumulated knowledge
2. **Context Trimming learns user patterns** — new tool starts cold
3. **Daily Digest becomes a habit** — missing it feels like losing a morning ritual
4. **Auto-tag creates taxonomy** — years of organized tags don't transfer
5. **Consult/Merge history improves routing** — new instance starts dumb
6. **Team spaces (Enterprise)** — social lock-in, others depend on your data

## What This Means for the Product

- **Free tier must be genuinely useful** — not crippled. Users need to experience the Memory Palace and one AI skill to understand the value.
- **Starter is the "hook"** — $5/mo is cheaper than a streaming service. Auto-tag alone saves 30 min/week.
- **Pro is the money tier** — all skills, unlimited notes, priority models. Most users who stay 3+ months upgrade here.
- **Enterprise is the long game** — not until Phase 3, but the pricing is already baked in.

## Open Questions for Gerald

1. **50% off for Year 1 or just 3 months?** — 3 months is safer for cash flow. 12 months maximizes adoption.
2. **Should Starter exist?** — Some argue Free → Pro only. Starter helps capture price-sensitive users.
3. **Lifetime deal?** — $399 one-time for early supporters. Creates evangelists. Limited to first 500 users.
4. **Usage-based component?** — Maybe Pro includes 100 AI actions/month, $1 per 10 after that? Adds revenue from power users without raising base price.
5. **Annual discount percentage?** — 10% is conservative. 20% ($16/mo effective) drives more annual commits.
