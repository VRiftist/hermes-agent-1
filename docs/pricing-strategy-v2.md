## Revenue Model

### Final Pricing Structure

| Tier | Monthly | Annual (15% off) | Included AI | Notes |
|------|---------|-------------------|-------------|-------|
| **Free** | $0 | $0 | None, local only | 50 notes, 2K context |
| **Starter** | $8 | $72/yr | 4 skills | AI compose, auto-tag, 8K context, 500 notes |
| **Pro** | $20 | $180/yr | All 7 skills | Full AI, 16K context, unlimited notes, memory palace |
| **Enterprise** | $50/user | Custom | All + custom skills | Teams, admin, API, priority models |

### Launch Discounts
- **Months 1-3**: Pro at $12/mo (40% off) — drive early adoption
- **Months 4-6**: Pro at $18/mo (20% off) — growth phase
- **Month 7+**: Full $20/mo — steady state
- **Lifetime deal**: $399 one-time for early supporters (limited)

### Why This Works
- $8 Starter < Netflix impulse buy threshold → high conversion
- $20 Pro competitive with Notion AI total ($20/mo with add-on)
- Capacities charges $8/mo and includes AI — we match that
- Claude's $200-300/mo targets enterprise only; we capture both markets
