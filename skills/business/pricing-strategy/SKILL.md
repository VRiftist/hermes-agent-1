# Pricing Strategy — LumenHub (v1)

## Market Context (2025-2026)

| Competitor | Free Tier | Paid | AI Pricing | Target |
|-----------|-----------|------|------------|--------|
| Obsidian | Free (self-host) | $50/yr Sync | None | Power users |
| Notion | Free (limited) | $10-15/mo | $10/mo add-on | Teams/enterprise |
| Logseq | Free (OSS) | $10/mo Cloud | Usage-based | Developers |
| Roam Research | Free (5MB) | $15/mo | Limited | Researchers |
| Bear | Free (basic) | $1.49/mo | None | Apple writers |
| Craft | Free (limited) | $9.99/mo | $9.99/mo add-on | Creatives |
| Capacities | 100 objects free | $8/mo | Included | PKM enthusiasts |
| Mem0 | Free (self-host) | $20/mo | AI memory layer | AI-forward users |

## Key Insight: The "AI Tax" is Real

Users pay MORE for AI but only if clearly valuable:
- Notion AI: $10/mo ADD-ON on top of $10 base = $20/mo total
- Craft AI: $9.99/mo ADD-ON = nearly doubles cost
- Capacities: $8/mo INCLUDES AI — perceived better value

## Final Pricing Structure (RECOMMENDED)

| Tier | Monthly | Annual (15% off) | What You Get |
|------|---------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Free** | $0 | $0 | Local only, basic notes, 50 notes, no AI |
| **Starter** | $8 | $60/yr | AI compose, auto-tag, 8K context, 500 notes |
| **Pro** | $20 | $180/yr | All AI, unlimited notes, 16K context, memory palace, priority models |
| **Enterprise** | $50/user | Custom | Teams, admin panel, API access, custom models |

## Launch Strategy (Year 1)

- **Months 1-3**: Pro at $12/mo (40% off) — early adopter pricing
- **Months 4-6**: Pro at $16/mo (20% off) — growth phase
- **Month 7+**: Full pricing ($20/mo) — steady state
- **Annual bundle**: Always 15% off (protects revenue vs 50% discount)
- **Lifetime deal consideration**: $399 one-time for early supporters

## Why NOT $200-300/mo (Claude's Suggestion)

Claude's $200-300/mo targets enterprise ONLY. For personal PKM this is too high — users will resist. Better to split: $20/mo personal Pro + $50/user/mo enterprise. Captures both markets without pricing out individuals.

## Key Metrics to Track

1. **Free → Starter conversion** (target: 5-8%)
2. **Starter → Pro conversion** (target: 15-20%)
3. **Monthly churn** by tier (target: <5% for Pro)
4. **AI usage per user** (actions/day/week)
5. **ARPU** by tier and overall
6. **LTV** — Pro user should exceed 12 months
7. **NPS** at day 30 (target: >40)
8. **Feature usage heatmap** — which AI features drive conversion?
