### Pricing Gap Analysis
- No competitor offers local-first AI as default
- AI is always an add-on or usage-based — never included in base
- LumenHub "AI included in paid tiers" is a key differentiator
- $8-20/mo range is competitive; below enterprise, above freemium
- Capacities at $8/mo sets the benchmark for indie PKM pricing
