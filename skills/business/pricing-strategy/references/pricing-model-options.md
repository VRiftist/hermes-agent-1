## Three Options Evaluated (2026-05-25)

### Option A: Pure Freemium (SELECTED)
- Free: Local only, no AI, 50 notes
- Starter: $8/mo — AI compose, auto-tag, 8K context
- Pro: $20/mo — All AI, unlimited, memory palace
- Enterprise: $50/user/mo — Teams, admin, API

### Option B: Aggressive Launch Pricing
- Starter: $5/mo (launch) → $8/mo (normal)
- Pro: $12/mo (launch) → $20/mo (normal)
- Trade-off: 3-5x adoption spike but delayed revenue

### Option C: Usage-Based
- Free: Unlimited notes, pay-per-AI-action
- Credits: $1/100 actions, $5/mo for 1,000
- Pro unlimited: $25/mo
- Trade-off: Higher ceiling but complex billing

### Decision Matrix
| Factor | A (Freemium) | B (Aggressive) | C (Usage) |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|-----------|
| Adoption speed | Medium | High | Medium |
| Revenue ceiling | Medium | Delayed | Highest |
| Simplicity | High | High | Low |
| MRR predictability | High | Medium | Low |

**Winner: Option A** for launch. Option C worth revisiting at 50K+ users.
