## Launch Discount Strategy (2026-05-25)

| Phase | Months | Pro Price | Discount | Goal |
|-------|--------|-----------|----------|------|
| Early Adopter | 1-3 | $12/mo | 40% off | Build base, collect testimonials |
| Growth | 4-6 | $16/mo | 20% off | Scale to critical mass |

- CAC vs LTV ratio tracked monthly by acquisition cohort
