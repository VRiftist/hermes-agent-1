# Product Launch Strategy — LumenHub

## Brand Positioning

**"Your knowledge, your AI, your rules."**

LumenHub is the first local-first AI-powered personal knowledge management system. Your notes, your memory, your intelligence — never in someone else's cloud. Powered by Hermes Agent with deliberate multi-model routing.

**Taglines:**
- "Think locally. Remember everything."
- "Your second brain, running on your hardware."
- "AI that stays on your side of the desk."

## Target Audiences

| Segment | Pain Point | LumenHub Value |
|---------|-----------|----------------|
| Power Users (Obsidian/Roam) | Tools powerful but dumb — no real AI | Full AI in existing PKM workflows |
| Enterprise Teams | Notion expensive, data leaves company | Self-hosted, local-first, data sovereignty |
| Privacy-First Users | Paranoia about AI reading notes | Local by default, cloud opt-in |
| Developers | Want PKM APIs to build on | Open-source core, plugin architecture |
| Students/Researchers | Information overwhelm | AI synthesis + spaced repetition + memory palace |
| Creative Professionals | Need inspiration + structure | AI creative partner with full work memory |

## Launch Phases

### Phase 1: "The Reveal" (2 weeks pre-launch)
- Teaser posts: "local AI in action"
- Channels: X/Twitter, HN, Product Hunt, Reddit
- Goal: Build waitlist, collect emails

### Phase 2: "Early Access" (2 weeks)
- Behind-the-scenes "how we built Hermes"
- Developer communities, Discord, Newsletter
- Goal: 500 beta testers, testimonials

### Phase 3: "Launch Day" (1 week)
- Full reveal, demo videos, comparison posts
- Product Hunt day 1, HN, all social
- Goal: Product Hunt top 5

### Phase 4: "The Story" (ongoing)
- User stories, case studies, feature deep-dives
- Blog, newsletter, podcast appearances
- Goal: Sustained momentum, enterprise inquiries
