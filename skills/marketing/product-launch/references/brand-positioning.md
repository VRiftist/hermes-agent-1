## Brand Positioning — LumenHub

### Core Narrative
LumenHub is the first local-first AI-powered PKM that treats your intelligence as yours — not training data for someone else's model.

### Positioning Statement
**For** knowledge workers who take their intellectual life seriously,
**LumenHub is** the only PKM that runs AI entirely on your hardware by default,
**Unlike** Notion, Obsidian, and Logseq which require cloud APIs for AI features,
**LumenHub** gives you enterprise-grade AI with personal privacy — no data leaves your machine unless you choose it.

### Brand Pillars
1. **Privacy by default** — Local AI first, cloud only when you choose
2. **Intelligent memory** — Your AI actually remembers across sessions
3. **Deliberate architecture** — 6-tier context, multi-model routing, quality gates
4. **Open ecosystem** — Works with your existing tools, not a walled garden
5. **Made for builders** — Developers, researchers, creators who think deeply

### Voice & Tone
- Direct, technical, confident — not hype-driven
- "We built this for ourselves and it works"
- Show, don't tell — demonstrate with real architecture details
- Respect the user's intelligence; don't dumb down

### Visual Identity Notes
- Dark theme primary (developer-friendly)
- Amber/gold accent color (intelligence, warmth)
- Clean geometry — not playful, not corporate
- Memory Palace visualization as signature UI element
