- "Notion sends your notes to OpenAI. We don't."
- "Notion charges $10/mo + $10/mo for AI. We include it from $8/mo."
- "Notion's AI is a feature. Ours is the foundation."

**vs. Obsidian:**
- "Obsidian is powerful but dumb. We add AI that actually understands your vault."

**vs. Logseq:**
- "Logseq is great for outliners. We're built for thinkers who need AI synthesis."

**vs. Roam Research:**
- "Roam pioneered networked thought. We bring AI into the network."

**vs. Craft:**
- "Craft is beautiful. We're both beautiful AND intelligent."
- "Craft's AI costs another $10/mo. We include it."
- "Craft is Apple-only. We're cross-platform from day one."

1. **"Your AI never sees your data (unless you let it)"** — Local-first by default
2. **"5 models, one engine"** — Deliberate multi-model routing
3. **"It remembers what you told it last Tuesday"** — Persistent memory palace
4. **"Your context doesn't overflow, it evolves"** — Active 6-tier trimming
5. **"From startup to enterprise, one platform"** — Free → $50/user/mo
